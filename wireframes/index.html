  • Swap is dimmed/locked — no accidental language switch mid-utterance

  • Card groups everything: pulsing dot, "LISTENING" label, "438 chars left", waveform, stop
  • No ring on stop button — clean, uncluttered, easy to hit at bedside
  • Card lifts slightly with shadow — feels like a persistent recording toolbar
  • Transcript has maximum room to grow before it touches the card edge
  • Closest to your sketch concept

  • Transcript lives inside a speech bubble (green-tinted, soft border, bubble tail)
